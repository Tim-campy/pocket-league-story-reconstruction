# Joint reconstruction of named switch selector locals

This is a source-only calculated candidate for `form.SubForm::DrawMenu(kairo.unity.ui.Graphics,System.Int32)->System.Void` (2,067 original IL bytes). Its whole game body remains UNKNOWN until the historical compiler and full verifier accept it. No game assembly was compiled to prepare this proposal.

The original has 34 locals and no switch opcode; the current body has 32 locals and reuses temporary slot31 for three recovered switches on `array2[0]`. Original aliases have distinct slots28/30/32 and unique case lists [0,1,2,3], [6,8], [12,14,15,16]. Each has one store, and every load is a dispatch comparison. Their lifetimes are1318?1491,1565?1613,1679?1822. The own stores are1318/1567/1698. A backward stack-dependency calculation proves each original selector expression has exactly the same instructions and operands as its own actual compiled counterpart; no fixed instruction window or candidate DTO is fabricated. The lists of cases uniquely bind regions, while source order and real own temporary stores bind the three source switches.

The candidate converts all three dispatches to if/else chains and gives each a separate named Int32 alias in the same lexical block. Each selector still evaluates once. Only terminal switch-owned breaks are removed. Nested break/continue/switch ownership, default or grouped labels, nonliteral labels, unknown stack effects and nonbijective provenance are refused. Changing one dispatch in isolation creates a complete but wrong local signature; the joint candidate instead has COMPLETE and exact34-slot prediction from the unchanged frozen smcs model. Its synthetic local names reconstruct slot identity; they are not claimed to be original variable names.

Mono evidence is pinned to Unity commit b6587926c0f146c8b12985744f33a13b39a22d57. `mcs/mcs/statement.cs`3830?3866 always creates a `LocalTemporary` for a nonconstant switch selector, stores it, and releases it after emitting the dispatch. `mcs/mcs/codegen.cs`357?392 retrieves and returns temporary locals by type. That mechanism explains how three source switches share one late slot. Named locals are declared by block insertion order, so three named aliases at their original lexical positions produce three interleaved slots instead.

The independent smcs fixture compiles three toy assemblies. A source with three side-effecting selector calls and recycled switch storage becomes the independently authored six-local target (own four locals), with aliases1/3/5. Full instructions, header, locals and EH are exact. Nested loop-owned break and nonbijective case provenance are negative cases. Source AST extraction precedes inspection of fixture IL. Reports contain the receipts.
